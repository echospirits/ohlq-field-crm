# Supplies status

Mode: Operate. Existing Neat visual system and canonical UI/UX guidelines apply.

The owner wants a quick ordering and delivery status board for materials flagged by Dx5 or confirmed Production replenishment orders. Ollie is a future signal source; Neat does not duplicate inventory forecasting or lead-time planning. A quote or supplier identity alone does not establish a Production purchase.

One compact card per material/location and replenishment cycle. Each card shows the material, location, current order/delivery state, available sourced arrival and originating signal/date. Payment plans and conditional supplier promises are labeled separately from arrival estimates. Dismiss is the only operational control. No tabs, history, record editors, catalog maintenance, filters or manual entry. Connection setup remains separate.

The explicitly requested cards are self-contained status decisions, an intentional exception to the default comparison-table guideline. Use a single column on phones and responsive two/three-column composition on desktop, shared semantic tokens and modest borders. No nested cards, decorative imagery, metrics or introductory banner.

Repeated usage alerts update one cycle; they never duplicate a card or revive its dismissal. Relevant completed delivery removes the card without inventory or receipt writes. Later new alerts can start another cycle. Preserve uncertainty, partial shipments, returns, unknown arrival and incomplete mailbox coverage.

Verify 390 x 844 and 1440 x 900 together: populated, empty, incomplete/error coverage, read-only access, dismiss pending/error/success, duplicate alerts and delivery lifecycle. One initial visual inspection and at most one correction/confirmation pass.
