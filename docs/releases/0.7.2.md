## Fixed

- Daily wholesale imports can recognize the supplier names saved on orders when sales reports identify those suppliers by vendor code. The bridge uses exact shared product codes and requires a unique supplier identity.
- Matching can use explicitly stored customer permit aliases while preserving location suffixes. Nearby dates, similar names or a shared permit family do not establish an alias.
- Manually filed orders remain protected after they become Completed. Ambiguous or unresolved historical supplier metadata cannot release their evidence to another order.

Sent and Paid remain manual. Automatic Filed still requires the exact A-3a store, a report date from the sale date through seven calendar days later, the complete product and bottle-quantity set, one unambiguous order/evidence pairing and unused report evidence. Original order snapshots and PDFs remain intact.
