# 0.13.0

Status: release candidate; production promotion pending.

## New

- My Schedule combines Day and Week planning, quick scheduling and reassignment, and contextual Worklist actions. Agency and researched Wholesale hours flag potential scheduling conflicts.
- Target Account keeps organization-specific account priorities available throughout core CRM workflows.
- The optional Sales Status / Pipeline pilot tracks relationship stages, buying state, status history, and accounts needing attention.
- Agency hours can be maintained manually or researched from source-backed public information through the Account Research workbench.

## Improved

- Compact sidebar navigation, collapsible Pipeline groups, and direct account address and telephone links improve field use.
- Opportunity scoring places more weight on supported traffic evidence, review volume, private dining, patios, and verified hotel meeting capacity.
- Wholesale order checklist controls show pending feedback while saving.
- Administrators can see the application version in Environment diagnostics.

## Fixed

- The Target Account choice in Log Visit stays within narrow phone screens.

- Scheduler safeguards reject stale or duplicate submissions and invalid local times, preserve account context, and resolve Calendar conflicts using source timestamps and ETags.
- Release reconciliation preserves production OHLQ login retries, upstream-block diagnostics, and optional direct wholesale order grants.
