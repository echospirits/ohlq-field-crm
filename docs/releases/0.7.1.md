## Improved

- Physical Visit capture saves first, then offers **Complete related work?** when eligible account work exists. Complete one, complete all, or leave open; dismissal never completes tasks.
- Ohio Agency numbers appear beneath the account name. Current organization inventory is visible near the top of Agency Overview, including stock, minimums, status and freshness.
- Reactivation Follow-ups use the latest qualifying Visit's active user, then the organization's own configured fallback. Existing owners, qualification windows and completed history are preserved.

## Fixed

- Removed the unnecessary post-Visit **Mark actioned / Keep open** Opportunity decision while retaining Opportunity records, links and independent business logic.
- Overdue/undated Schedule items align consistently, long names wrap and mobile Worklist controls stay within the viewport.
- Explicit task completion revalidates tenant/account/assignment/status, preserves normal history/calendar effects and safely handles retries. Repeated/concurrent imports cannot duplicate a new Reactivation lapse condition.
