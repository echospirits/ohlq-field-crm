# Neat 0.15.5 — Retail Intelligence sales

## Improved

- Compare 7-, 30-, and 90-day bottle sales across every Retail Intelligence recommendation filter and in Product opportunities on retail account pages.
- Each row labels partial or unavailable 90-day history separately from a confirmed zero. When the sales and recommendation dates differ, the row shows the date through which the 90-day sales were counted.

## Release scope

The production patch applies the Retail Intelligence change from TST `8bbb1548bc29674d0406c480c3aef459f2d8781b` to production base `3e9f25a94edabb0fdccb7897046dc9e55ef95880`. The displayed 90-day total uses retained Echo counter and wholesale bottle sales, including returns, through the latest complete sales report on or before the recommendation’s inventory snapshot. Existing tenant product scope and daily coverage records govern the totals. This release requires no migration, backfill, dependency change or configuration change.
