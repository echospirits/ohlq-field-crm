# Neat 0.10.1 — Production Supplies reliability

Released application `b75da7a2987d6bb95c71fde16b77cad5ae01d068` as **[v0.10.1](https://github.com/echospirits/ohlq-field-crm/releases/tag/v0.10.1)** to production and the integrated hotfix to TST on November 13, 2026. Both deployments are READY and healthy. Exact runtime/card/tag acceptance is recorded in the [validation](0.10.1-validation.md) and [checklist](0.10.1-checklist.md).

## Improved

- Confirmed Production replenishment purchases can create Supplies cards without a Dx5 alert. Quotes alone and unrelated purchases stay excluded.
- Cards clearly separate payment plans and supplier promises from shipment and delivery evidence, while preserving the simple card-and-Dismiss interface.
- Exact non-shared material orders can match when the inventory alert omits its location. Completed-delivery hiding continues to require physical, quantity and timing proof.

## Fixed

- Signature images and quoted correspondence no longer create repeated purchases. Reprocessing can quarantine unsupported legacy candidates while retaining source and audit history.
- Repeated trusted Dx5 labels keep one card when extraction splits a parenthetical fragment into an assumed ID. Existing catalog mappings, raw facts, cycles and dismissals remain intact.
- Imports resume without repeatedly extracting the same attachments when their provider download handles change.
- Authenticated buyer requests, supplier acknowledgements and identifying documents enrich one purchase while preserving mixed invoice boundaries and original units.
- A durable worker owns the 15-minute import schedule, with bounded catch-up, persisted tenant ownership, deployment fencing and recovery watchdogs.
