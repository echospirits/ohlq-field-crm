# Neat 0.14 — user update

## New

- Dark mode saves to your account and follows you across mobile and desktop.
- Forgot password? lets you securely reset your password from the sign-in screen.
- Organization admins can review user logins and active days in the new User activity report.

## Improved

- Core CRM now includes Pipeline, Analytics with CSV exports, and Direct Wholesale Orders.
- Account pages show outstanding team tasks below Notes + contacts, with quick actions to log a visit, complete, reschedule, edit, reassign, or cancel.
- Wholesale Intelligence uses current account assessments with clearer priorities, recommended next actions, effort, confidence, and data coverage. Product strategy is editable in Organization Setup. Intelligence remains an add-on.
- Google Calendar scheduling has stronger retry and conflict handling, safer reconnect and cleanup, and clearer Check and sync now feedback.

## Fixed

- Accounts without a saved sales status clearly show Not set instead of appearing targeted.
- Expanded visit photos stay centered on mobile, lock background scrolling, and return you to your original position when closed.
- Customer-facing organization presentation settings are kept consistent during setup and editing.
