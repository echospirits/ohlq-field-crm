See [plan](0.9.2-plan.md), [validation](0.9.2-validation.md) and [checklist](0.9.2-checklist.md). Preserve v0.9.1 and earlier refs.

## Improved

- An authorized manual forecast refresh downloads warehouse inventory again, including later changes on the same business day. The scheduled daily job keeps its existing reuse behavior; demand and delivery retain their daily snapshot semantics.
- Accepted inventory retrievals append to history. Forecasting and My Schedule balances select the newest validated product/warehouse observation; prior quantities and evidence remain intact.
- Failed, stale or incomplete retrievals retain the last good stock with explicit unavailable/retained status. Affected pairs have no actionable balance, and the manual refresh is incomplete. Expected native source-ineligible items remain explicitly unavailable.

## Fixed

- The manual workflow and ingestion no longer reuse the first inventory observation of the day. A timestamped unique key allows newer observations without rewriting earlier facts; exact-instant replays and older exports cannot duplicate facts or demote newer checks.
