## Data and dashboard boundaries

This adapter includes **no coverage-receipt writer** and launches **no backfill**. The separate Codex task must preserve its current main-only job, add validated atomic receipts for historical and zero-sale dates, and maintain them during future daily loads/corrections. Existing production facts alone cannot be certified. The copied dashboard now reads that same main database; the original dashboard retains its existing data sources until certified history and reconciliation justify cutover.

The empty/partial status of production reporting is a data-coverage outcome, separate from connection success. Do not load or synchronize TST to make a preview appear populated, and do not weaken the first-gap or matched-year completeness gates. Preserve unrelated data, schedules, historical refs and existing Bailment reporting during promotion and rollback.
