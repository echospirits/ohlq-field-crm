# Dashboard experience

Audit baseline: TST `5faee9df930bd76b5e56b669f58a57210527f7d5`, inspected 2026-10-10.

My Schedule prioritizes today’s execution and existing Worklist. New recommendations must show the decisive reason and a contextual action. Keep management reporting available for deeper desktop work rather than overwhelming the field dashboard.

The dashboard puts eligible warehouse shortfalls above the personal day or week. Route planning follows the Worklist calendar. Search and any unassigned team work precede the always-visible Explore opportunities and Team activity and performance sections. The organization-wide events and staff schedule follows those insights.
